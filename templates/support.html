You're Not Alone 💚

Taking care of your well-being starts with small steps and asking for support when you need it.

Remember: You don't have to handle difficult feelings alone. Talk to a trusted adult, school counsellor, or qualified mental health professional.

Helpful Support Resources

Tele-MANAS

India's national tele-mental health service provides mental health support and guidance.

Call: 14416

Visit official website

Child Helpline

Children who need care, protection, or help during a difficult situation can contact India's Child Helpline.

Call: 1098

Visit official website

Emergency Assistance

If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent emergency assistance in India, contact the national emergency response service.

Call: 112

Visit official website

Trusted People

You can start by speaking with someone you trust, such as a parent, teacher, school counsellor, or another supportive adult.

You can ask them to listen, help you find support, or accompany you to a professional.

Small Steps for Everyday Well-Being

  • Keep a regular sleep routine.
  • Take short breaks when schoolwork feels stressful.
  • Spend time on activities you enjoy.
  • Talk to someone instead of keeping everything inside.
  • Ask for professional help when difficulties persist.
